😟 The problem

When records live only on computers inside one hospital, a failed computer, a disaster or a virus can make them hard to reach, and another branch cannot easily see them. Data also keeps growing.

☁️ The solution

A cloud-based patient information system lets authorized staff at connected branches reach the same records, with backups, and with storage that can grow.

🔐 Security matters

Cloud does not mean everyone can see everything, and it does not make data automatically safe. Hospitals need login, authorization, privacy rules, backups, monitoring and responsible data management. In this model, the Receptionist role cannot open medical details, and each record view is logged.

This model shows the idea at small scale. A real hospital system would need individual staff accounts, encryption and legal compliance.
